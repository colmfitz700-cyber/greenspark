Ireland has an opportunity.

Clean power.Local opportunity.Smarter infrastructure.

Ireland's appetite for digital infrastructure is growing. So is the need for clean electricity to run it. GreenSpark's proposal explores how renewable generation and an AI-powered data centre could work together, while weighing energy, water, carbon and community impact from the start.

A proposal, not an established fact. Everything on this site describes a concept under evaluation. Any numbers shown are illustrative and labelled as such.

How this model works
  • Wind generation and demand are indices where 100 is the illustrative baseline for each.
  • Only part of wind output lines up with demand in time. The model assumes 80% of generation can be matched directly.
  • Whatever demand is not matched comes from the grid, and the renewable share of that grid supply is the fourth slider.
  • Water intensity falls as efficiency measures rise. Carbon impact follows the non-renewable grid energy left over.
  • Every coefficient is a placeholder chosen to show behaviour, not to predict it.
